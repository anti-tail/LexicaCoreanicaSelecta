The data on this site are made from the following resources. 우리말샘 and 한국어기초사전 are published by the National Institute of Korean Language (국립국어원) under CC BY-SA 2.0 KR; the dialect data of Lee (2015) are published under CC BY 4.0.

| Resource | Parts used | Provider |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 우리말샘 | Headwords and definitions; historical data (forms by century, citations, sources); dialect entries (forms, regions, definitions and the modern entries they are linked to) | National Institute of Korean Language <https://opendict.korean.go.kr/> |
| 한국어기초사전 | English equivalents (headword translations and translated definitions) | National Institute of Korean Language <https://krdict.korean.go.kr/> |
| Lee (2015) dialect data | Dialect forms of 14 regions of the peninsula (dialect search and map; most forms are from Ogura Shinpei, *Chōsengo hōgen no kenkyū*, 1944) | Lee, Sean (2015) A Sketch of Language History in the Korean Peninsula. *PLoS ONE* 10(5): e0128448. CLDF edition <https://github.com/lexibank/leekoreanic> (CC BY 4.0) |

- The outline of the dialect map is simplified from Natural Earth (public domain, <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/>). The representative points of the regions were set by the Lexica Coreanica project and are not in the original resources.
- The forms of Lee (2015) are written in a near-IPA transcription. Rewriting them in Hangul and linking the concepts to modern entries of 우리말샘 were done by the Lexica Coreanica project and are not in the original resource.
- The dialect “word groups” (dialect entries linked to the same modern entry; unlinked entries grouped by identical definition and part of speech) and the English names of the regions were made by the Lexica Coreanica project and are not in the original resources.
- The “Citations” page (`corpus.html`) makes the citations quoted in the historical information of 우리말샘 searchable in full text, with identical sentences merged (`data/u/cites.json`). The matching keys (absorbing spelling variation) were produced by the Lexica Coreanica programs and are not in the original resources.
- Segmentation and the Yale romanization (for reference) were produced mechanically by the Lexica Coreanica programs and are not in the original resources.
